## Hi, I'm Marta 👋

I'm a behavioural scientist who designs experiments and evaluates policy.

I use survey experiments and causal inference to study the mechanisms behind human behaviour, especially how people interact with technology. My recent work looks at **persuasion and mobilisation campaigns**, especially digital ones, and at **political and epistemic efficacy**: whether people feel able to understand, and to influence, the world around them.

**Currently:** Fellow, London School of Economics · DPhil, University of Oxford

### 🧪 Methods
- **Experiments:** factorial and conjoint designs; multi-stage experiments with multiple randomisations for mediation analysis, run with human participants and with AI model evaluations
- **Quasi-experimental designs:** difference-in-differences, event studies, interrupted time series
- **Policy evaluation**
- **Survey methods:** survey weighting, stratified models

**Tools:** R · Python · Git
